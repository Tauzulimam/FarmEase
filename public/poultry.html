Farm Setup & Breeds

Housing in Different Climatic Zones

  • Arid/Semi-arid: Use N-S oriented sheds with high vents, reflective roofs, and cooling systems.
  • Hot-Humid: Elevated floors on stilts with open mesh sides for maximum airflow.
  • Cool/Temperate: South-facing, insulated sheds with heating systems for winter.
  • Monsoon Prep: Elevate feeders, ensure dry bedding, and complete pre-monsoon vaccinations.

Operations & Health

Beginner Tips & Benefits

  • Benefits: Quick income, nutrition security, and employment opportunities.
  • Stocking Density: 4-5 layers/m², 8-10 broilers/m².
  • Feed Management: Provide balanced rations (Layers: 16-18% protein, Broilers: 20-22% protein).
  • Biosecurity: Limit visitor access, disinfect, and quarantine new stock.
  • Vaccination: Follow schedule for Marek’s, New Castle, and Gumboro disease.
