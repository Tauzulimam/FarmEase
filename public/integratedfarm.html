Poultry-Fish System

Concept: Poultry droppings directly fertilize the fish pond, promoting plankton growth which serves as a natural food source for fish.
Poultry Component: 500-600 broiler chickens (e.g., Vencobb 400) can be housed above or adjacent to a 1-hectare pond. Housing should be well-ventilated and waste directed into the pond.
Fish Component: Polyculture of Indian Major Carps like Catla (surface feeder), Rohu (column feeder), and Mrigal (bottom feeder) is ideal to utilize all pond niches.
Synergistic Benefits: Reduces fish feed cost by up to 60%, ensures efficient waste recycling, and provides diversified income from both eggs/meat and fish.
Estimated Net Profit: Approximately ₹2.5 - ₹3.5 Lakhs per year from a 1-hectare system, representing a 40-60% increase over standalone farming.

Cattle-Fish System

Concept: Cow dung is used as a primary organic fertilizer for the fish pond. The slurry from a biogas plant using the dung is even more effective.
Cattle Component: 5-6 dairy cattle can provide enough manure for a 1-hectare pond. Shed washings and slurry can be channeled into the pond.
Fish Component: Composite fish culture with 3-6 species of carps is recommended. Regular liming is required to maintain water pH.
Synergistic Benefits: Provides income from milk and fish. Biogas production from dung saves energy costs. Pond slurry can be used to fertilize adjacent fodder crops.
Estimated Net Profit: Around ₹3 - ₹4 Lakhs annually from the combined enterprise, boosting farm profitability by 30-50%.

Pig-Fish System

Concept: Pig excreta is an excellent fertilizer. Pigs are housed on the pond embankment, and the waste is directly drained into the pond.
Pig Component: 30-40 pigs can be integrated with a 1-hectare pond. Pig sties should be constructed with a slope towards the pond.
Fish Component: Air-breathing fish like Catfish (Magur, Singhi) are often preferred as pig waste can lower dissolved oxygen levels.
Synergistic Benefits: Extremely high productivity. Reduces feed cost for both pigs (who can eat aquatic weeds) and fish. Pigs attain market weight faster.
Estimated Net Profit: Can be highly profitable, with estimates ranging from ₹3 - ₹5 Lakhs per year from a 1-hectare system due to the high value of pork.

Duck-Fish System

Concept: Ducks live on the pond surface. Their droppings fertilize the water, and they control unwanted insects and weeds while aerating the water with their movement.
Duck Component: 200-300 ducks can be raised per hectare of water area. A simple, low-cost shelter on the embankment is sufficient.
Fish Component: A polyculture of carps is ideal. Ducks eat predatory insects and snails, which benefits the fish.
Synergistic Benefits: No additional feed required for ducks as they feed on pond organisms. Provides income from duck eggs, meat, and fish simultaneously.
Estimated Net Profit: A well-managed system can yield a net profit of ₹2 - ₹3 Lakhs per hectare annually.
Video Recommendation: Watch Duck-Fish Farming Video

Goat-Fish System

Concept: Similar to cattle integration, goat manure is used as organic fertilizer for the fish pond. This system is ideal for semi-arid regions where goat rearing is common.
Goat Component: A unit of 20-30 goats can support a 0.5 to 1-hectare pond. Goat sheds are built on embankments with channels for waste collection.
Fish Component: A polyculture of carps is suitable. Goat manure is rich in nutrients, promoting excellent plankton blooms.
Synergistic Benefits: High profitability from goat meat and milk, supplemented by fish sales. Excellent for areas with limited grazing.
Estimated Net Profit: Given the high profit margin of goat farming, this integrated system can yield net profits upwards of ₹3 - ₹4.5 Lakhs per year.
