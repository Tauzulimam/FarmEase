Goat Farming

Profitability

~50% gross margin annually, which can increase to 80% if home-grown feed is utilized. The overall livestock sector shows a strong CAGR of 12.99%.

Rearing Requirements

  • Space: 4-5 sq ft per adult in a shed.
  • Feed: 2-4% of body weight daily (green fodder & concentrate).
  • Waste: Manure is excellent for biogas and organic fertilizer.

Climate-Adapted Care

Provide shaded yards in hot zones, ensure good ventilation in humid areas, and use deep-litter bedding for insulation in cool climates. Maintain a buck-to-doe ratio of 1:25.

Dairy (Cattle & Buffalo)

Profitability & Growth

Approximately 25-35% profit margin for smallholder dairy operations. Key growth rates (CAGR) include Milk (5.62%), Egg (6.87%), and Meat (4.85%).

Rearing Requirements

  • Space: 8-10 sq ft per adult in a shed plus an open yard.
  • Feed: 2.5-3% of body weight (60% roughage, 40% concentrate).
  • Waste: Cow dung is ideal for biogas plants and vermicompost.

Top Producer States (Total Livestock)

  • Uttar Pradesh: 68.7 m
  • Rajasthan: 56.8 m
  • Maharashtra: 49.6 m

Climate-Adapted Care

Use north-south oriented sheds in arid zones, elevated floors in humid zones, and insulated walls in cool zones. Vaccinate against FMD & HS before monsoon.
