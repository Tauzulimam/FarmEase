Summary & Strategic Takeaway

India's agricultural sector in 2025 showed strong fundamentals with diversification into high-value crops driving farmer income growth. The shift toward premium crops, technology adoption, and export orientation positions the sector for sustained growth in 2026 and beyond.

Key Takeaway

Farmers focusing on high-value crops with technology integration and market linkages can achieve 3-5x higher incomes compared to traditional cereal farming, making 2025 an opportune year for agricultural transformation.
